## Verified facts (TEXTUAL page)

- Format: "All papers must be written in English, formatted according to the official IEEE Conference
  Proceedings guidelines (two-column layout), and submitted electronically via the workshop submission portal."
- Categories:
  - "Full Research Papers" "UP TO 8 PAGES": "Mature research contributions, thorough empirical investigations, or validated technical methodologies."
  - "Short / Vision Papers" "UP TO 4 PAGES": "Work-in-progress, novel research directions, preliminary empirical results, or visionary challenge proposals."
  - "Tool & Dataset Papers" "UP TO 4 PAGES": "Demonstrations of automated text processing pipelines, mining tools, or annotated open-access software text corpora."
- Deadlines: "All deadlines are AoE (UTC-12h), unless otherwise stated by SANER." Abstract submission October 19, 2026;
  Paper submission October 23, 2026; Author notification December 8, 2026; Camera-ready January 8, 2027; Workshop March 9, 2027.
- Submit Now links to `easychair.org/conferences/?conf=saner27tracks`.
- Contact: textualw@gmail.com.

## Verified facts (SANER workshops track page)

- TEXTUAL shares the EasyChair site `saner27tracks`. Dates match the TEXTUAL page.
- The two-page limit on that page applies to proposals to organize a workshop, not to individual workshop papers.
- "The workshop proceedings will be included in a separate section of the conference's proceedings."

## Decisions taken under these facts (see decision_log.md)

- Category: Short / Vision Paper (up to 4 pages): a small pilot with preliminary empirical results. Not a full paper.
- Page budget: the whole PDF, references included, is kept to 4 pages, which satisfies the strictest reading of "up to 4 pages".
- Template: IEEEtran `conference` class, unmodified fonts and margins.
- Anonymity: no anonymity rule is stated. The named-author PDF is the default; an anonymous PDF is compiled from the same
  source as a fallback. The designation stays conditional until an organizer or EasyChair instruction says otherwise.
- Deadline: 23 October 2026 AoE = 24 October 2026, 15:59:59 Asia/Dubai. Team target: upload on 23 October (UAE).
